# User testing kit

A short session (about 20 minutes per person) to find out whether ClearView helps
dyslexic readers. Even **3–5 participants** give useful evidence and real quotes.

## Before you start
- **Who:** people who are dyslexic or find reading difficult. Note age range and
  whether they normally use any reading tools. Don't record names in your results;
  use P1, P2, …
- **Consent:** explain what the app does, that photos of objects are sent to
  Cloudflare for a description and are not stored, that they can stop at any time,
  and that you are testing the app, not them. Get their agreement (follow your
  school's ethics rules if it has them).
- **Set-up:** a phone with the ClearView site open, and a table with 6–8 everyday
  objects, including at least one with a printed label (a drink bottle, a box).

## Tasks
Give each task out loud, then watch without helping. Note where they hesitate.

1. **Find your colours.** "Open Reading preferences and pick the background colour that is
   most comfortable to read." *(Record which tint they choose and why.)*
2. **Name one object.** "Point the camera at the bottle and find out what it is."
3. **A busy scene.** Put 3–4 objects together. "Find out what's closest to you, and what
   else is there."
4. **Listen.** "Have the result read aloud to you, and follow along."
5. **Read a label.** "Use Read a page to read the label on this box."

For each task record: completed (yes / with help / no), roughly how long it took,
and anything they said.

## Questionnaire
Ask after the tasks. Use a 1–5 scale: 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree.

1. The text on the screen was easy to read.
2. Changing the background colour made reading more comfortable.
3. The spacing between letters, words and lines suited me.
4. Highlighting each word while it was read aloud helped me follow along.
5. The app told me what the object was correctly.
6. It was easy to understand what to do on each screen.
7. I would use this app in everyday life.

Then ask openly:
- What was the most helpful part?
- What was confusing or frustrating?
- What would you change?
